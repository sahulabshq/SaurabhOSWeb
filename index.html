Do more with your phone than scroll

Saurabh OS is one app for studying, coding, reading, listening and keeping your files in order, so you stop switching between ten apps.

Get the appSee what it solves

Everyday problems, solved in one app

Each tool answers something that usually needs a laptop or a different app.

Your phone is full of apps, and none of them work together.

Everything starts in one calm place

A single home screen shows your battery, storage and recent projects, then opens every tool with one tap.

Learning to program usually means waiting until you can use a laptop.

Write and run code on your phone

Write a program, press Run and read the result right below it. Errors show up in a Problems list, so you know what to fix.

Many courses and tutorials expect a command line.

Run commands without a computer

Use a full command line and install new languages and tools from the in-app store, with no setup guide to follow.

Long PDFs are tiring on a small screen, especially on the move.

Read less with your eyes, more with your ears

Open any PDF or note and let it read aloud. Change speed, pitch and voice, and set a sleep timer.

Finding one lecture among hundreds of files wastes time.

Your videos and songs, sorted

Browse video and audio in separate lists, search by name, and keep private items in a hidden vault.

You need a smaller file, a different format or just the audio, and the phone has no easy way to do it.

Trim, convert and tidy media

Split, resize, change speed, add text, pull out the audio or convert a video before you share it.

Projects, PDFs and downloads end up scattered across the phone.

Your files, under control

Browse by folder, sort by recent, and send items to the Trash first so a wrong tap is never final.

A phone gets slow or warm and you cannot tell why.

See what is slowing your phone

The Task Manager lists running apps so you can stop the ones you do not need. A battery saver switches on by itself when power runs low.

Security tools are usually hard to find and harder to trust.

Check your own network and websites

Create strong passwords, check a site’s security headers, look up a domain and inspect a file, only on systems you own or may test.

All your tools, one tap away

Open the launcher and pick what you need. Everything shares the same clean look.

  • Code editor and command line
  • PDF reader with Listen mode
  • Video and audio player with tools
  • Files, Trash and Task Manager
  • Camera and sensor tools
  • Cybersecurity Lab
