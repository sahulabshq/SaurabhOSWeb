Privacy policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Draft for review. Have a qualified lawyer review this policy, including under India’s DPDP Act, and update it whenever the app changes.

Your privacy comes first

Saurabh OS is designed and written by Saurabh Sahu (“Saurabh”). Saurabh protects your privacy by keeping your data with you.

  • Saurabh does not collect any data from the app. Your files, projects and settings stay on your phone.
  • No analytics. The app does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No accounts. You never have to sign up or log in.

What stays on your phone

Your files, browser history (private tabs are not saved), settings and hidden vault stay on your phone. The hidden vault is encrypted and unlocks with your screen lock or fingerprint. The app turns off Android’s automatic backup, so it does not copy your data to the cloud.

When the app uses the internet

The app connects only for things you start, such as installing items from the store, opening web pages and running network checks, and for first-time setup, which downloads the Linux system from official Debian sources. These go to servers run by others, not to Saurabh, and their own privacy policies apply.

This website

The website has no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

Deleting your data

Your data is on your phone, so you control it. Delete files in the app or uninstall Saurabh OS to remove everything. If you email Saurabh, he uses your message only to reply and will delete it if you ask.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
